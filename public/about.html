Our Mission

Boldly Leading Others
to Encounter Jesus

Our mission is clear: to boldly lead others to encounter the life-changing love of Jesus. Unbound by denominational labels, we stand united in our devotion to the one true King.

The Experience

What Is an
Encounter?

The Encounter is a 3-day life-changing event in a camp atmosphere designed to get you free from the distractions of everyday life. This is a safe place where you can draw closer to God and be freed from the chains that bind as you come to the realization of the call God has placed on your life.

The attendee will experience the practical love of Jesus poured out like never before — in an atmosphere conducive to experiencing freedom from religion and the wiles of this world. You'll build new personal relationships and leave with a greater passion to serve God.
