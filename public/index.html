What is an Encounter?

Three Days to
Break Every Chain

Just as Jesus rose in three days, three days in an Encounter can transform what years of struggle could not. We create a safe, confidential space — welcoming to all — where the Holy Spirit does what only He can do.

Men and women alike leave different. Not fixed by a program, but free because of a personal encounter with Jesus Christ.

1
Arrive & Open
Let your guard down. Be welcomed. Begin the journey.
2
Go Deeper
Spirit-led sessions. Breakthrough. Real freedom begins.
3
Walk It Out
Celebrate. Commission. Go change the world.
